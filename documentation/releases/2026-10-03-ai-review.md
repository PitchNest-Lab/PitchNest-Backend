# Release notes: AI review and persona tuning (3 Oct 2026)

**Area:** AI · **Branch:** `preston/ai-review-persona-tuning` · **Prompt version:** `2026-10-03.fair-challenge`
**Tech Week task:** #4 "AI Review & Testing". Reduce persona toughness: challenging, not harsh.

## Summary

The panel was harsh because of several stacked instructions, not one setting. This release keeps the panel rigorous but makes it fair at every level. It also fixes three bugs found during the full-flow review.

## What changed

### 1. Persona toughness (behavior change)

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Top slider tier: "EXTREMELY DEMANDING… low patience… interject after ~15 s" | "DEMANDING BUT FAIR": high bar, always respectful; normal 30 s interrupt rule |
| "TOUGH: push back at least once on any unsupported claim" | "CHALLENGING": push back, but accept a credible answer the first time |
| A vague number "is never accepted: the same panelist follows up **until** there is a number" | Follow up **at most twice**, then log it as an open concern and move on; "we don't know yet" closes the thread |
| Formal "I'm out" unlocked at toughness **70** | Unlocked at **85** (and Shark Tank), never in the first half, and only after the founder had a chance to answer |
| "Leaning out" from toughness **40** | From **50** |
| Panel identity "Marcus (lead/**skeptic**)" | "Marcus (lead)… investors who want this founder to succeed — rigorous, never hostile" |
| Shark Tank: "combative… a little harsh… visibly lose patience" | "Playfully tough… high-stakes, never cruel"; steers long answers back with humour |
| Growth VC Marcus: "blunt, impatient" · Seed Marcus: "blunt" | "Direct and efficient" · "Direct and candid" |
| Contradictions flagged "🚨 Prioritize probing this!" | "Ask the founder to confirm — may be a mis-hearing or an updated number" |
| Coach (Riley) inherited the "extremely demanding" tier at high slider values | Coach is capped at the realistic tier |
| No slider value → 60 | No slider value → 50; garbage input is clamped to 0–100 |

**New: fair-challenge rules**, injected at every level for every persona:

- challenge the claim, not the person;
- at most two follow-ups per point;
- "don't know yet" is a valid early-stage answer;
- acknowledge good answers;
- one concern per turn;
- treat odd figures as a possible mis-hearing first.

### 2. Report scoring

- Added **score anchors** to the panel and coach evaluators (0–20 absent … 41–60 typical early pitch … 81–100 investor-ready). Topics the session never reached are not deducted unless core. Risks are phrased as fixable.
- "6 tough investor questions" → "6 realistic investor questions (a mix of hard and medium)".
- Deck Check one-liner: "blunt" → "candid, constructive".

### 3. Bug fixes

- **Verdicts mis-split when a panelist mentioned a colleague.** "Marcus: I agree with Sarah that… I'm in" was cut to "I agree with", and Sarah got the rest, which could flip the in/out colour. Verdicts are now split only at `Name:` boundaries, with the old loose split as a fallback (`src/utils/verdictSplit.ts`).
- **Verdict audio was synthesized one panelist at a time.** It's now concurrent, so the founder waits for the slowest verdict instead of all three added together.
- **Organization dashboard showed almost every pitch as 100 / "Excellent".** `pitchScoreFromReport` summed four 0–100 categories (capped at 100), and `readinessFromReport` multiplied readiness by 4. Both now use the same 0–100 mean as the PDF report.

### 4. Versioning

- Every saved report now includes `evaluation_report.prompt_version`, so we can compare sessions before and after a prompt change and filter data for future fine-tuning.

### 5. Testing

- `npm test` now runs three suites: memory/security, the **new persona tone guardrails** (140 prompt builds covering every persona × level × mode, plus verdict-split and dashboard-score tests), and the deck-type tests (which existed but were never wired in).
- **New `scripts/persona-eval.ts`:** a live end-to-end persona eval against the real model that reports harsh-language hits, stacked questions, acknowledgement rate, early walk-outs, verdicts and scores per persona.
